How do you handle returns or exchanges?

We make returns and exchanges simple by coordinating the pickup, transportation, and delivery of returned items. Our team ensures every return is processed efficiently while keeping you informed throughout the process.

What does business consulting do?

Our logistics consulting service helps businesses optimize their shipping operations, reduce transportation costs, improve supply chain efficiency, and select the best delivery solutions for their needs.

Can I cancel a shipment after it's been booked?

Yes, you can request a cancellation before the shipment has been dispatched. Once the package is in transit, cancellation may not be possible, but our support team will help you explore available options.

Can you assist with customs clearance procedures?

Absolutely. We provide customs documentation support and guidance to help ensure your international shipments comply with import and export regulations, reducing unnecessary delays.

What is your delivery policy?

We are committed to delivering every shipment safely and on time. Delivery times vary depending on the destination and shipping method selected, and all shipments can be tracked in real time until they reach their destination.
